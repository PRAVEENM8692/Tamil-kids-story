## 01 hook
November 8, 2026. Diwali night. Samayapuram, Tiruchirappalli.
The whole town was full of firecracker noise.
That night, two 17-year-old boys went to a hospital to see a friend burned by firecrackers.
Right next to that hospital, they had no idea their lives were about to end.
That night, the police had no lead on who killed them.
But in just three days, six people were arrested. One of them was brought back from Thoothukudi, 300 km away.
How? And the bigger part is what happened the next midnight.
Watch till the end.

## 03 intro
The two who died were Rahul and Vishal. Both were 17.
They were relatives. They lived by doing daily-wage work.
Rahul had lost his parents. He was staying at a relative's house in Inam Samayapuram.
Vishal was the son of Raja, from the same town.
The spot was the Pullambadi canal bank near the Samayapuram toll plaza.
Right beside it is a big medical college hospital.
That hospital is where this story begins.
## 04 investigation
In that hospital, a friend named Vignesh was being treated for burns from Diwali firecrackers.
Rahul, Vishal and six other friends went to see him.
While returning after the visit, about 80 metres from the hospital, an argument began.
It was around 11:30 at night.
News reports say a gang then arrived and attacked Rahul and Vishal with sickle-like weapons.
Passersby were the ones who informed the police.
When Inspector Gunasekaran rushed there, Vishal was already dead.
Rahul was very badly wounded.
He died on the way to the hospital.
Two boys who went to visit a friend in hospital lost their lives right near the hospital.
This is the pain of this case.
## 05 clue
So, why did this happen?
The Tiruchirappalli Superintendent of Police said Rahul had a personal grudge with one of the arrested over a small matter.
The initial investigation says that is the reason for the murder.
But the news agency ANI and some Tamil media tell a different side.
It was reported that the police said there was a feud over a girl between Rahul and one of the arrested, named Keni.
One Tamil report also says some people had teased a girl from the area.
So is the reason one, two, or are both the same thing?
There is still no firm answer. This is only the initial stage of the investigation.
So please do not spread speculation about the boys who died.
Only the court can say what is true.

## 07 answer
The answer is the camera. CCTV. First, Rahul's brother filed a complaint.
It was registered at Samayapuram police station as Crime No. 386.
Immediately, Tiruchirappalli SP Dongre Praveen Umesh formed three special teams.
Those three teams combed through the CCTV recordings from the area.
Police say they identified the suspects from the faces seen there.
Among them, they identified one named Sivanesan as the main accused.
But he was not in Tiruchirappalli. They learned he had gone to Thoothukudi.
About 300 km away. The special team went there, found him, and brought him back to Tiruchirappalli.
In the end, six people were arrested. Sivanesan is 21.
Keni is 19. Saravanan is 18. Kirubakaran is 19.
Hariharan is 22. Another one is 20 years old.
His name differs between reports as Renison and Karanison.
Six young men between 18 and 22.
Note this: however far they ran, what gave them away was a camera at the very place they fled from.
## 08 arrest
But the story does not end here. After the arrests, another big incident happened one midnight.
Tuesday night, November 10. The weapons used in the murder had to be recovered.
For that, police took Sivanesan to the very spot of the incident.
This is what the police side says. There he suddenly picked up stones and threw them at the police.
A constable named Mohan was injured. The police vehicle's window glass cracked.
One report also says he tried to attack with a weapon he had hidden.
Even after a warning he did not stop. So Inspector Gunasekaran fired his gun.
The bullet hit his left knee.
The police say they fired in self-defence and to stop him from escaping.
The injured constable and Sivanesan were taken first to Srirangam Government Hospital, then to Tiruchirappalli Government Hospital.
The SP went straight there to see them. This is the police side's account.
For such incidents there are separate inquiry procedures.
We do not know how that will end.
