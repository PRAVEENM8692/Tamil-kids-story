## 07 answer
The answer is very simple. A man's eyes.
The bus driver's name is Madheshwaran. One passenger was fully covered in a cap and a mask.
His behaviour did not feel right to the driver. He informed the police at once.
Some reports say it was the bus conductor. Whoever it was, that tip was the turning point.
The Adhiyamankottai police team followed the bus for about five kilometres.
Just after midnight, on the Sesampatti bypass road in Dharmapuri district, near a bakery, they stopped the bus.
Seeing the police surround the bus, Nishanth jumped out of a window and ran.
But Sub-Inspector Vignesh and Head Constable Murugan caught him around one thirty after a short chase.
Dharmapuri Superintendent of Police Arun Kabilan informed Madurai Police Commissioner Rajendiran.
A special team from Madurai was already waiting near Salem.
They brought Nishanth to Madurai.
He was questioned for about four hours at Adhiyamankottai police station.
Based on what Nishanth told them, the police recovered about six kilograms of jewellery hidden in Madurai.
Reports differ on the place. One report says Vilacheri.
Another says the foot of the Kapaleeswari Amman temple hill in Madakulam.
Reports say over eleven kilograms in total has been recovered.
Some reports say everything has been recovered.
But another report says the police have not confirmed that.
The Superintendent of Police praised the driver, the sub-inspector and the head constable.
