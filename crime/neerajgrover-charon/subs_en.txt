## 01 hook
May 7, 2008. A newly rented flat in Malad, Mumbai.
At 7:30 in the morning, a man comes and knocks on the door.
That evening, a borrowed car leaves Mumbai and heads into the forest.
Two bags on the back seat. Then, on the phone of a missing young man, a call is picked up at the wrong moment.
Just one call. And it turns the whole case around.
This is the Neeraj Grover case. Stay with me till the end.
## 02 disclaimer

All the pictures are drawn depictions.
## 03 intro
Neeraj Grover was a young man who came to Mumbai from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh.
He worked in the creative department of a TV production company.
Maria Susairaj came from Mysore. A girl who knew dance.
Between 2002 and 2004 she acted in three Kannada films.
Not one of them did well. So she came to Mumbai for the third time, looking for chances.
That was April 29, 2008. Neeraj had been helping her get a chance on TV.
He even arranged auditions for her. But Maria already had a boyfriend.
Emile Jerome Mathew, a lieutenant at the naval base in Kochi.
They were from the same hometown. They were talking of an engagement within two months.
## 04 disappearance
May 6. Maria moves into the newly rented flat in Malad.
Neeraj skips a gathering with friends and goes to help her at eleven at night.
That night, Jerome calls her many times from Kochi.
On the last call, he hears a man's voice in the background.
He asks, "Who is there?" That same day, without taking leave, he leaves the base and catches a flight to Mumbai.
May 7, 7:30 in the morning. There is a knock on the door.
Jerome finds Neeraj in the bedroom.
An argument, a fight. The allegation is this: Neeraj is stabbed with a kitchen knife.
In that fight, Maria's hand is also injured.
Neeraj dies right there. At ten in the morning, Neeraj's mother in Kanpur starts calling him.
Nobody picks up.
## 05 clue
Now the cover-up begins. One in the afternoon.
Maria borrows a friend's car. She goes to a mall nearby.
Bags, room freshener, new curtains, a bedsheet, a knife.
She buys everything. The court accepted that the two of them cut up the body and packed it into bags.
They wash the room of that flat with soapy water and acid.
Half past four in the evening. While the building's watchmen are watching, they put two bags in the back of the car.
On the way, they buy petrol at a petrol pump.
They buy a lighter at a roadside shop.
Near Manor, inside the forest, they burn the bags.
At nine thirty at night, as if nothing had happened, they return home.
The next day, when a friend of Neeraj asked, Maria said, "Neeraj left at one thirty.
He left his phone behind."
Then, along with that friend, she goes to the Malad police station and even files a missing complaint.
This one thing has always amazed me.
For ten days, she kept repeating this same lie.

## 07 answer
The answer is all three. First, the phone. Neeraj's phone was with Maria herself.
About one hundred and thirty calls came to it and nobody answered.
Except for one call.
On the way to the forest, inside the car, while taking the phone out of her jeans pocket, she accidentally picked up a call.
She cut it at once. But the police traced the location of that call.
It was on the road leading toward the forest.
Her claim, "I went shopping in Dadar," turned into a lie right there.
Even before that, Neeraj's father Amarnath had been pasting posters all over the city looking for his son.
Seeing that, the Crime Branch started a separate investigation.
Next, the car. First she said, "Jerome's friend Jitesh gave the car."
He was then at sea on a ship. He refused to lie.
Then she gave the name of the real owner of the car.
Third, the wall. To hide the bloodstains, only one wall of that bedroom had been freshly painted.
All the other walls were the old colour. One more thing.
From May 7 to 20, Jerome had phoned Maria almost a thousand times.
This call record added even more to the suspicion.
## 08 arrest
May 21. The police search the Manor forest.
Bone fragments, burnt cloth, beads, a button, a chain, an armband.
They find everything. The same day, the police hold a press conference and announce the case.
The police bring the car, its owner and the witnesses to the police station.
Seeing all of it with her own eyes, Maria breaks down.
She tells what happened. She said that it was Jerome who killed Neeraj.
Both are arrested. Jerome had first told the police, "I came to Mumbai for naval training."
There was no record of that anywhere. May 25.
The Crime Branch finds a nine-inch knife inside the garbage chute of that residential complex.
Another mystery remains unsolved even today.
That night, from eleven thirty to a quarter to twelve, eight calls on Neeraj's phone were answered by someone.
Who it was has never been clearly stated.
## 09 court
In this case, the prosecution brought forty-eight witnesses.
The watchmen who saw the bags being loaded. The petrol pump worker who sold petrol to Jerome.
The roadside shopkeeper who sold the lighter to burn the body.
Jerome's friend, who said he was angry when he left.
July 1, 2011. The Mumbai sessions court delivers its verdict.
Emile Jerome: ten years in prison for culpable homicide not amounting to murder.
Maria Susairaj was cleared of the murder charge.
But for destroying evidence, she got three years, the maximum punishment.
The court did not accept this as a planned murder.
It said it was a fight that happened in a sudden rage.
Maria had already spent three years in jail during the trial, so she walked out the very next day.
Neeraj's father called this verdict "freedom for Maria."
This verdict drew a lot of criticism.
A woman lawyer said, "The judge has looked at it with the old view that a lover's betrayal can drive a man to kill."
The state, the family and Jerome appealed to the High Court.
I could not find clear news about the final outcome of those appeals.
## 10 ending
One thing is clear in this case.
A wrongly answered call, a borrowed car, a repainted wall gave away those who tried to hide a murder.
The newspapers wrote three hundred pieces.
The judge rejected that as a claim far from the records.
Inspired by this case, a Hindi film also came out in 2011.
But there is another side too. A young man lost his life going to help a friend.
His mother called at ten in the morning, and nobody ever picked up.
