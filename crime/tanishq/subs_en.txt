## 01 hook
12 November 2026. Madurai. Just a few days after Diwali.
A famous jewellery showroom, newly opened. At 9:30 that morning the staff open the shop.
On the first floor, jewellery boxes lie scattered.
The jewellery that was inside is gone. Over 15 kg of gold, plus diamond jewellery.
The value is reported to be 21 crore rupees.
When it became clear who had been caught up in this, Madurai was stunned.
A young YouTuber. His father. And his friend, an Instagram influencer with lakhs of followers.
Let's see how this case turned around in just two days.
Watch till the end.
## 02 disclaimer
This is a real case that is still under police investigation.
As this video is made, the main accused has not yet been caught.
No court verdict has come. So everyone mentioned here is only an accused person.
I am only telling you what the police said and what the news reported.
All pictures are illustrations.
## 03 intro
First, let's look at the shop.
It is on the Palanganatham-Kalavasal bypass road in Nehru Nagar, near Madurai railway station.
A three-storey building. Parking on the ground floor.
Gold jewellery on the first floor. Work on the diamond and silver sections was still going on upstairs.
Note one thing here. The shop had opened only a few days earlier.
Construction work was not fully done. According to the news, there was no locker facility to lock the jewellery away.
The jewellery stayed in the display cabinets. On such a night, early on 12 November, this incident happened.
## 04 investigation
How did they get in? The reports differ a little on this.
But look at what one report says.
At 3:34 in the morning, the person climbed the iron scaffolding around the building, reached the third-floor terrace, and went down through an unfinished lift shaft, that report says.
The police side says he stayed inside for about an hour, packed the jewellery into bags, and left on a scooter.
Another report says he broke in through the back wall.
The details of how they entered differ.
But it is certain that the first-floor jewellery went missing.
How much was lost? Some reports say over 15 kg of gold, many diamond pieces, worth 21 crore in all.
In some other reports the amount differs a little.
The police moved in at once. Forensic experts and sniffer dogs.
The Police Commissioner came and looked in person. About 25 shop employees were questioned.
Construction workers who worked nearby were questioned too.
Suspecting the thieves may have fled by train to Kerala or the northern states, alerts went to railway stations as well.
Special teams were formed under Deputy Commissioner Inigo Divyan.
## 05 clue
What did the police look at first? The shop's CCTV recordings.
Something turned up there. In the days before the theft, a young man had visited the shop like a customer.
Two or three times. A different set of clothes each time.
But the same coloured cap. One hand bandaged.
The police believe his aim was to study how the jewellery was displayed.
A shop employee recalled that such a suspicious young man had come and gone.
Later, he pointed him out from the recordings, police sources say.
Puthiya Thalaimurai reports one more detail.
A tattoo on his hand. As a customer, on another day in a cap, and during the theft.
The same tattoo is visible in all three recordings.
The news says that is how they confirmed his identity.
That young man is Nishanth. From the Madakulam area of Madurai.
According to the reports, a video editor, a YouTuber, and a bike-taxi driver.

## 07 answer
The police followed another lead.
Some local people say they saw Nishanth and his father burning clothes in a field in Madakulam.
News reports say the police suspected these could be the clothes worn during the theft.
Then, the mobile phone clue.
After the theft, a call went out from Nishanth's phone, from the Thiruppalai area of Madurai.
That call went to a woman named Meenakshi Nachiyar.
Who is Meenakshi Nachiyar? According to the reports, she is an Instagram influencer.
Over one lakh followers, and about 1.81 lakh according to some reports.
She promotes many products online, including cosmetics.
Another interesting detail. She and Nishanth met while studying visual communication at The American College in Madurai.
Nishanth dropped out midway, but the two stayed close friends.
Some reports say the two also ran a YouTube channel together.
One report calls her his girlfriend.
## 08 arrest
14 November 2026, Saturday. Two days after the theft.
Madurai police arrested Suresh and Meenakshi Nachiyar.
The news says they were traced by following mobile phone signals.
The police say about 5 kg of gold jewellery was recovered from them.
Another report says 6 kg. Look at what one report says.
Nishanth, the day after the theft, asked his father Suresh to come near the Thiruparankundram bridge.
The police side says the two then buried the jewellery in a plot in Madakulam.
The police have said that the stolen jewellery was divided into three shares.
One share with the father, one with Meenakshi, and Nishanth escaped with the other.
Reports also say a bag buried in a pit at the foot of the Madakulam hill was found at a spot Meenakshi Nachiyar pointed out.
With this, about 15 kg of jewellery in total has been recovered, the police say.
Now an important question. Why this theft?
This is what one media outlet says is a police-side report.
That Nishanth did it to help Meenakshi build a house, the police say.
The same outlet says one more thing. Reports say Meenakshi also took part in the planning talks.
But that report does not say who said so.
Other reports only say that she received a share of the jewellery, and that Nishanth contacted her after the theft.
They say the police are still looking into whether she helped sell the jewellery or helped in any other way.
What her side is, I do not know even now.
So I will not draw any conclusion about her role.
After medical tests, both were produced before the judge.
Judicial custody was ordered until 29 November, that is fourteen days.
They were sent to Madurai Central Prison.
## 09 court

The police say the remaining jewellery is with him.
