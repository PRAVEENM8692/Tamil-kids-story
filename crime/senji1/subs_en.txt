## 02 disclaimer
This is a case that is still unsolved and under investigation.
No one has been declared guilty in it.
It is not our intention to blame anyone.
We tell it based only on press reports.
Where reports differ, we say so.
All pictures are illustrations. If new information comes, we will tell it in the next part.
## 03 intro
This story takes place in Alampoondi village near Gingee in Villupuram district.
Some reports name the nearby Nadupattu village instead.
The girl's name is Hemalatha. Some reports call her Hemavathi.
She is seven years old and studies in Class 2 at a government school.
Her parents are daily-wage workers.
Reports say it is a family connected with farm labour and brick-kiln work.
October 11. Her seventh birthday. Relatives came.
They cut a cake. An ordinary, happy day.
That night everyone went to sleep as usual.
In the house where her parents and grandmother slept, Hemalatha slept too.
## 04 investigation
October 12, around 3 am. When the parents woke up, the child was not there.
First they searched around the house and in the street.
There was no answer. The same day, they filed a complaint at the Sathyamangalam police station.
Here is an important point.
One neighbour has said that at 3 am he heard a child crying out for her mother three times.
Another piece of information.
One report says a 75-year-old woman who lives alone in a field about 200 metres from the house said someone knocked on her door twice at 3 am.
But reports say the family says they heard nothing.
Investigators are looking into this difference.
Here is a truth we must know.
Police officers always say the first few hours matter greatly when searching for a missing child.
If a clue turns up within that time, the chance of finding the child is higher.
## 05 clue
Sathyamangalam police registered a case and began the search.
According to news reports, more than a hundred policemen joined the search.
Drone cameras, sniffer dogs and forensic experts all came.
They searched wells, ponds, water bodies, farmland and forest areas.
But no solid clue turned up. Then the police focused on some suspects.
When they looked at their mobile phone traces, one name came up.
It was an astrologer from Thanjavur.
Reports say he had stayed in that village, near the girl's house, for about two weeks.
When the police phoned him to call him in for questioning, reports say he suddenly stopped talking and switched off his phone.
Here is a warning. This is only a piece of investigation information.
No one has declared him guilty. No evidence has come out so far that he is behind this disappearance.
## 06 question
Here is the question we ask now. How does a child vanish from a house while her parents are asleep?
One possibility is that someone came from outside and took her.
Another possibility is that the child went out by herself.
But where would a seven-year-old go, in the middle of the night, alone, calling for her mother?
Third, it could be the work of someone who knows everything.

## 07 answer
But ten days passed. The search found nothing.
People began to get angry.
Accusing the police of being lax, many political parties and villagers started protests.
The opposition DMK staged a road blockade.
Other parties, including the PMK, also said the child must be found quickly.
That pressure grew big.
Similarly, in Karnataka's Cyanide Mohan case, it was a protest that made the police investigate seriously.
But this case is different.
## 08 arrest
October 22.
The Tamil Nadu DGP took this case from the local police and transferred it to the Crime Branch CID, the CB-CID.
The CB-CID is the special wing of the Tamil Nadu police that investigates complicated cases.
Cases left unsolved locally mostly come here.
October 23. The CB-CID team's second day.
They drew a map of the area around the girl's house.
They questioned the parents and relatives.
So far no one has been arrested in this case.
No one has been declared guilty.
