## 01 hook
June 24, 2015. A headless body lies beside a railway track in Namakkal district.
But the very next day, a video of the young man speaking starts circulating on WhatsApp.
"I am going to kill myself." How can a dead man speak on video?
In the meantime, the police uncovered a big truth.
This is the Gokulraj case. Stay with me till the end.
## 02 disclaimer
One important note. This is a case on which the court has given its verdict.
The High Court has upheld it as well.
I only tell what court judgments and news reports say.
The intention is not to insult anyone.
Some details are taken from news reports of that time.
I tell them as they are. All pictures are drawn depictions.
## 03 intro
Gokulraj, a 21-year-old young man. An engineering graduate.
Tiruchengode is a temple town. The Arthanareeshwarar temple on the hill is very famous there.
June 23, 2015. Gokulraj goes to that temple with his friend Swathi.
Swathi belonged to a different community. The family says Swathi said they were not lovers.
This one thing was about to turn into a danger to his life.
## 04 disappearance
This is the allegation. Some men come to the temple.
They say "Yuvaraj wants to see you" and take Gokulraj away.
Yuvaraj is the head of a community organisation in that area.
Swathi said later that they took her phone and told her "get out of here".
Next morning, his headless body is found on the railway track near Pallipalayam.
The post-mortem reportedly showed he was strangled, with a stab wound on the neck.
The case was first registered as a suspicious death.
Only later was it changed to a murder case.
## 05 clue
Now the video. A video and a note, saying Gokulraj killed himself, spread on social media.
But how did that video come after he was killed?
The police say he was threatened and made to write and speak before he died.
The allegation is they tried to build a story of suicide after a failed love.
News reports say police found the phone that first recorded the video belonged to one of the accused.
That means the phone that made the lie also broke the lie.
## 06 question
Now the question. Where would people who tried to turn a murder into a suicide make a mistake?
In a jeep, in a number plate, or on a white sheet of paper?
Tell me your guess in the comments. Then keep watching.
## 07 answer
The answer is all three. Here is what the reports say. Yuvaraj reportedly borrowed a jeep from a friend and went to the Sankagiri hills.
The police seized that jeep.
On the very day of the murder, the jeep's number plate was changed.
The police got the new number plate design from the shop's computer.
The timestamp on it matched the time of the murder.
One more thing. Two white sheets were bought from the neighbouring shop to write the suicide note.
The shopkeeper identified him. Arun's call records and tower data showed where he had gone.
## 08 arrest
In the middle of this, another big twist.
DSP Vishnupriya, who was investigating the case, dies in her government quarters in September 2015.
The nation was shocked. Within days the case moved to the CB-CID.
By then Yuvaraj was on the run.
Reports say he kept moving for over a hundred days: Salem, Gulbarga, Puttaparthi, Payyanur.
During that time he released six audio messages and gave TV interviews.
He said he had nothing to do with the murder.
October 11, 2015. After over a hundred days, Yuvaraj surrenders at the Namakkal CB-CID office.
He was given a welcome then. An officer reportedly called it a big embarrassment for the police.
## 09 court
The case took another turn in court.
Of 72 witnesses, 21 turned hostile.
The key witness Swathi was one of them.
The High Court played the temple CCTV footage in front of her.
It asked who was in it. She identified Gokulraj.
But she refused to identify herself. So the court started contempt proceedings against her.
In this judgment the court also laid down a new procedure to handle CCTV and electronic evidence.
The judges visited the temple and the crime spot in person.
March 8, 2022. The Madurai special court gave life sentences to ten people, including Yuvaraj.
Yuvaraj got life sentences on three counts. Then June 2, 2023.
The High Court upheld the life sentences of eight people.
It reduced the sentences of two to five years.
The court said those people were in the grip of the demon called caste.
## 10 ending
One thing is clear in this case.
A number plate, a white sheet and a phone tower exposed those who tried to pass off a murder as suicide.
But there is another side too. A 21-year-old lost his life just for going to a temple.
When the verdict came, Gokulraj's mother spoke.
That no mother should ever suffer what she suffered.
